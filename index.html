✏️ After-Reading Tasks

A. Comprehension Questions

  1. Who is the narrator of the story? How old is he?
  2. Why does Holling have to stay after school with Mrs. Baker on Wednesdays?
  3. What do the other students do on Wednesday afternoons? Why does this make Holling feel left out?
  4. What is Mrs. Baker's attitude toward Holling at first? Find a quotation to support your answer.
  5. What does Mrs. Baker decide to do with Holling during their Wednesday afternoons?
  6. How does Holling feel about this plan? Why?
  7. What does Holling's father do for a living? How does this affect Holling's life?
  8. Describe the relationship between Holling and his sister. Give one example from the chapter.
  9. What is happening in the world outside Holling's school in September 1967? How does this affect the characters?
  10. Why do you think the author chose the title The Wednesday Wars?

B. Vocabulary Practice

Fill in the blanks with the correct word from the list:
(furious, reluctant, bar mitzvah, mutter, smirk, errand, betray, clench)

  1. Holling's sister was __________ when she found out he had to stay after school.
  2. Mrs. Baker was __________ to let Holling just sit and do nothing.
  3. Danny's __________ was coming up, and he was nervous about it.
  4. Holling had to __________ his fists to stop himself from showing his anger.
  5. Mrs. Baker didn't __________ — she spoke clearly and directly.
  6. The boys would __________ when they thought Mrs. Baker wasn't looking.
  7. Holling felt like his teacher wanted to __________ him in front of the whole class.
  8. Holling's mother asked him to run an __________ to the store.

C. Discussion / Speaking

  1. Fair or unfair? Do you think it was fair that Holling had to stay after school with Mrs. Baker? Why or why not?
  2. First impressions. What was your first impression of Mrs. Baker? Did it change by the end of the chapter?
  3. Family life. Compare Holling's family with a typical family today. What is similar? What is different?
  4. Historical context. The story is set in 1967 during the Vietnam War. How does this background affect the story so far?
  5. Prediction. What do you think will happen between Holling and Mrs. Baker in the coming months? Why?

D. Creative Tasks

  1. Diary entry. Write a short diary entry from Holling's point of view after his first Wednesday with Mrs. Baker.
  2. Letter. Write a letter from Mrs. Baker to a colleague explaining why she chose to work with Holling.
  3. Character map. Draw a diagram showing Holling's relationships with: Mrs. Baker, his father, his mother, his sister, and his friends. Add one adjective to describe each relationship.
  4. Role play. Act out a conversation between Holling and his sister after he tells her about the Wednesday arrangement.
  5. Newspaper article. Write a short article for the school newspaper about "What Happens on Wednesdays at Camillo Junior High."

E. Literary Analysis (for more advanced students)

  1. Find three examples of humor in Chapter 1. What makes them funny?
  2. How does the author use dialogue to show character? Find one exchange between Holling and Mrs. Baker and explain what it reveals about each of them.
  3. What is the setting of the story (time and place)? How does the setting influence the events?
  4. Find one example of foreshadowing in Chapter 1. What might it suggest about what will happen later?
